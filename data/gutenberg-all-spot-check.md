## Read by hand (2026-10-27)

All 50 descriptions are neutral: author, original language, subject or form, translator. None describes a plot. Every category fits the book. A few are arguable but not wrong:
- *Memoirs of Lady Fanshawe* is a জীবনী, though it is her own memoir.
- *Gleaner Tales* is filed as an উপন্যাস, though it is a book of stories.
- *Portrait Miniatures* is filed as ইতিহাস, as art history.
- *The Theory and Practice of Argumentation and Debate* is filed under শিক্ষা (education).

An earlier sample of 50, read the same way, found problems that were fixed in `scripts/gutenberg-all.ts` before these files were written:
- **Volume titles.** “Volume 2” came out as “Volume ume 2”.
- **Legends and folk tales filed as novels.** *Legends of the Middle Ages* is one example. Folk tales, legends and retold myths now go to পুরাণ ও লোককথা: 250 books.
- **Shelf-only categories.** Plays and poems were found from Gutenberg's bulk-assigned shelves alone: books on film, actors' memoirs and studies of poets were “plays”. নাটক went from 962 books to 804 and কবিতা from 1,749 to 1,573.
- **Bible commentaries filed as history:** 134 books.
- **Too many books in a generic অন্যান্য.** 698 had no subject; that is now 149, the rest placed by their Library of Congress class.
- **Hidden translations.** One of the 50 was a translation whose translator Gutenberg doesn't name: Einstein's *Fundamental Ideas and Problems of the Theory of Relativity*, given as a lecture in German. Translations are now also recognised from Wikidata's record of the language an author wrote in. That left out 68 more books (Tolstoy, Jókai, Sarah Bernhardt's memoirs, Marie Curie and others), with a hand-checked list of authors who wrote these books in English themselves.
